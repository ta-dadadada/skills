# Research provenance

This package distills two user-supplied Japanese research handoffs on productivity
UI (received 2026-09-05). The handoffs combine product examples, design-system
guidance, and the researcher's own synthesis. The links below preserve that
reading trail; their current contents were not independently audited for this
package. Instructions here are the package's conditional design judgments, not
verbatim vendor requirements. Consult current primary documentation before
relying on an exact product capability, numeric limit, or compliance claim.

| Source role in the handoff | Further reading | Package interpretation |
|---|---|---|
| Linear: interaction efficiency | [Method](https://linear.app/method/introduction), [selection](https://linear.app/docs/select-issues), [board layout](https://linear.app/docs/board-layout) | GUI first; accelerators for repetition; alternate representations of records |
| Attio: data-centered work | [Table views](https://attio.com/help/reference/managing-your-data/views/create-and-manage-table-views), [objects, lists, views](https://attio.com/help/reference/attio-101/attios-data-model/define-your-data-model-objects-lists-and-views) | Tables for comparison/editing; reusable views where justified |
| Carbon: component responsibility | [Data table usage](https://carbondesignsystem.com/components/data-table/usage/) | Align action placement with scope; disclose secondary detail |
| Primer: structure and surfaces | [Layout](https://primer.style/product/getting-started/foundations/layout/), [navigation](https://primer.style/product/ui-patterns/navigation/), [dialogs](https://primer.style/product/components/dialog/guidelines/), [action menus](https://primer.style/product/components/action-menu/guidelines/) | Preserve collection context; choose surfaces by task complexity; adapt narrow-screen interaction |
| Primer: completion and access | [Forms](https://primer.style/product/ui-patterns/forms/), [empty states](https://primer.style/product/ui-patterns/empty-states/), [table accessibility](https://primer.style/product/components/data-table/accessibility/) | Labels, actionable recovery, and accessible component behavior from design time |
| NN/g: rationale | [Heuristics for complex applications](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/usability-heuristics-complex-applications/) | Visible system status, recognition, control, efficiency, and error recovery |

The supplementary handoff also points to [UI accelerators](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/ui-accelerators/)
for alternate expert paths and [general usability heuristics](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/ten-usability-heuristics/)
for the review rationale. Its proposed cross-product screen survey is future
research, rather than evidence already collected in this package.

The calm visual language and the end-to-end workflow are synthesis. This package
deliberately leaves font sizes, radii, toolbar counts, and optional feature sets
to the product's design system and demonstrated needs.
